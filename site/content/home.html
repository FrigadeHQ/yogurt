FAQ

What kind of computer do I need?

A Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 14.4 or newer, a 64-bit PC with Windows 10 (version 2004) or newer, or a 64-bit Linux computer with Ubuntu 22.04 or newer. Each needs at least 8 GB of memory. If your computer is too slow, Yogurt tells you during setup. On Windows and Linux, you connect your calendar with a private calendar link.

Does Yogurt join my meetings?

No. Yogurt records your microphone and the sound playing on your computer. No bot joins the call, so let the other people on the call know you’re recording.

Are recordings saved?

Yes. Your recordings, transcripts, and notes are saved locally on your computer.

Is Yogurt free?

Yes. Yogurt is free and open source under the MIT license.

Which languages does Yogurt understand?

Yogurt transcribes 99 languages. English is the default and the fastest. For meetings in other languages, pick Multi-language in Settings, or from the language menu in a note, even during a call. In Multi-language mode, everyone can speak their own language, and Yogurt keeps up when someone switches languages mid-meeting. Summaries are written in the meeting’s main language, or one you choose.

Does Yogurt work offline?

Yes. After a one-time download of the AI models, transcription and summaries run locally on your computer.
